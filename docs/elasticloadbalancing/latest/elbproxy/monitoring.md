# Monitor a proxy

Elastic Load Balancing for Envoy (ELB for Envoy) publishes metrics and logs that you use to monitor the health and performance of your proxies. Each proxy sends metrics to Amazon CloudWatch (CloudWatch), and publishes its Envoy process logs to Amazon CloudWatch Logs (CloudWatch Logs) in your account. You can also collect Envoy statistics directly from a proxy.
