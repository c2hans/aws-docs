# Amazon Augmented AI

Amazon Augmented AI (Amazon A2I) enables you to build the workflows required for human review of ML predictions. Amazon A2I brings human review to all developers, removing the undifferentiated heavy lifting associated with building human review systems or managing large numbers of human reviewers.

## Developer Guide

- [Developer Guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/latest/dg/use-augmented-ai-a2i-human-review-loops.html?icmpid=docs_a2i_lp): Provides a conceptual overview of Amazon A2I. Offers step-by-step instructions for using AI applications such as Amazon Rekognition, Amazon Textract, or your custom machine learning (ML) models to create workflows required for human review of ML predictions.
