# Limitations and notes for the Close CRM connector
<a name="close-connector-limitations"></a>

The following are limitations and notes for the Close CRM connector:
+ Close CRM is supported as a source only. Writing to Close CRM is not supported.
+ Job bookmarks are not supported.
