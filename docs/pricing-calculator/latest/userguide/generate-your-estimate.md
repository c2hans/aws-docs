# Managing your estimates
<a name="generate-your-estimate"></a>

This section provides an overview of the features AWS Pricing Calculator provides for managing and organizing your cost estimates effectively.

You can create groups to categorize your estimates based on different criteria such as cost centers, product architectures, or AWS Regions. You can use groups to compare estimates side-by-side and make informed decisions about your AWS deployments.

You can also save your estimates by generating a unique public link for each estimate you create. Additionally, you can export your estimates as PDF or CSV files.

The following sections provide step-by-step processes for each feature.
