# AWS Documentation in Kindle Format

You can get many Amazon Web Services (AWS) guides for free on your Kindle.

You can use any of the following steps to download the Kindle version of a specific AWS guide:

- Go to the [AWS author page in the Kindle store](https://www.amazon.com/Amazon-Web-Services/e/B007R6MVQ6), choose the guide that you want, and then choose Buy to purchase it for free from the Amazon website.
- Go to the [AWS Documentation website](https://aws.amazon.com/documentation/), choose the AWS service that you want, choose the Kindle link for the guide that you want to use, and then choose Buy to purchase it for free from the Amazon website.
- For any guide that you have open in your web browser, choose the Kindle link in the upper-right corner of the webpage, and then choose Buy to purchase it for free from the Amazon website.
