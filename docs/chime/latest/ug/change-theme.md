# Switching visual modes (desktop client only)
<a name="change-theme"></a>

You can switch the desktop client between light and dark modes at any time.

**To switch modes**

1. Open the ellipsis menu next to your name and choose **Settings**.

1. On the **General** tab, under **Appearance**, select one of the options.
