# Finding meeting IDs
<a name="find-meeting-number"></a>

Look in the following locations for meeting IDs:
+ If the meeting appears on your calendar, open the meeting invitation. The ID appears under the link to the meeting.

  ```
  Click to join the meeting: https://chime.aws/0123456789
  Meeting ID: 0123 45 6789
  A headset is recommended or you may use your computer’s microphone and speakers.
  ```
+ If you don't have a meeting invitation, contact the meeting organizer or an attendee who has an invitation.
