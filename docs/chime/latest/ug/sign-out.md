# Signing out of Amazon Chime
<a name="sign-out"></a>

You can sign out of and back into Amazon Chime at any time. Remember to use your Amazon Chime credentials to sign back in.

**To sign out of Amazon Chime**

1. Open the ellipsis menu next to your name and choose **Sign out**.

1. Do one of the following:

   1. To sign out of the client that you currently have open, choose **Sign out**.

   1. To sign out of Amazon Chime on all your devices, choose **Sign out of all devices**.
