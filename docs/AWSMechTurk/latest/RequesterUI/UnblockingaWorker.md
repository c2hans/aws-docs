# Unblock a Worker
<a name="UnblockingaWorker"></a>

If you mistakenly blocked a Worker, you can unblock them.

**To unblock a Worker**

1. On the Mechanical Turk Requester website at [https://requester.mturk.com/](https://requester.mturk.com/), choose the **Manage** tab and then choose **Workers**.

1. On the **Manage Workers** page, choose the Worker ID of the Worker you want to unblock.

1. On the **Manage Individual Worker** page, choose **Unblock Worker**.

1. In the **Unblock Worker** dialog box, enter a reason for unblocking the Worker and choose **Unblock**.

 The Worker receives a message with the reason you are unblocking them.
