# Award a bonus
<a name="GivingaBonus"></a>

You can award bonuses to Workers to encourage them to keep working for you.

**To give a Worker a bonus**

1. On the Mechanical Turk Requester website at [https://requester.mturk.com/](https://requester.mturk.com/), choose the **Manage** tab and then choose **Workers**.

1. On the **Manage Workers** page, choose the Worker ID of the Worker you want to give a bonus to.

1. On the **Manage Individual Worker** page, choose **Bonus Worker**.

1. On the **Bonus Worker** page, enter the **Amount** of the bonus, the **Assignment ID** of the assignment that the Worker worked on, and the **Reason** for the bonus.

1. Choose **Pay Bonus Now**.
