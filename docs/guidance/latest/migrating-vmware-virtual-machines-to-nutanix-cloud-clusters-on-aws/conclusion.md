# Conclusion
<a name="conclusion"></a>

This implementation guide provided a mapping of various VMware products to their Nutanix equivalents, the use of the Nutanix Sizer to conduct assessments, and the process of migrating virtual machines from VMware to Nutanix Cloud Clusters (NC2) on AWS. For further information and next steps, Nutanix provides the following additional resources:
+ Nutanix's [test drive](https://cloud.nutanixtestdrive.com/login) program guides IT teams step-by-step through a migration with Nutanix Move.
+ Nutanix's [disaster recovery](https://www.nutanix.com/products/nutanix-cloud-infrastructure/disaster-recovery?utm_medium=redirect) product helps you to meet recovery SLA, reducing cost by as much as 70%. Nutanix offers a [30 day free trial](https://www.nutanix.com/uk/products/nutanix-cloud-clusters/aws/free-trial) of NC2.
+ Nutanix also has training partners and [Technical Certifications](https://www.nutanix.com/uk/support-services/training-certification/certifications) which are designed to recognize the skills and knowledge required to successfully deploy, manage, optimize, and scale you environment.
