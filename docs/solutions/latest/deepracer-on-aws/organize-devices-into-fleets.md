# Organize devices into fleets
<a name="organize-devices-into-fleets"></a>

With fleets, you can quickly pick the devices that are relevant to a given location. Only administrators can manage fleets.

To manage fleets, choose **Fleets** under **Device management** in the left sidebar. To create a fleet, enter a name, for example, "London", and a region. Then assign cars and timers to it. A device belongs to one fleet at a time. Reassigning a device moves it from one fleet to another.

![Creating a fleet with a name and region](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/solutions/latest/deepracer-on-aws/images/device-management/create-fleet.png)

You can edit a fleet’s name or region, and you can delete a fleet.

![The fleet list showing fleets and their assigned devices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/solutions/latest/deepracer-on-aws/images/device-management/list-fleet-view.png)
