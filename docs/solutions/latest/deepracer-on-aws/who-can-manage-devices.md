# Who can manage devices
<a name="who-can-manage-devices"></a>

The actions you can perform depend on your role.
+ Administrators can onboard (activate) devices, organize them into fleets, and perform all remote-control actions. Administrators can also delete devices.
+ Race facilitators can view devices and perform remote control during events, including restarting the service, triggering an emergency stop, and changing the tail-light color. Race facilitators cannot activate devices, manage fleets, or delete devices.
+ Racers have no device access.
