# Partner integrations
<a name="partner-integrations"></a>

SDMA integrates with third-party partner products available on AWS Marketplace. These integrations use SDMA’s connector framework to bring partner capabilities — such as 3D optimization, format conversion, and quality analysis — directly into your governed asset workflow.

Each partner integration is a pre-built solution with its own installation guide and CDK deployment. You deploy the partner’s infrastructure into your account, register the connector in SDMA, and approve it on your asset templates. From that point on, the integration runs automatically as part of your asset lifecycle.
