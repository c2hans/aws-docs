# Finding Your Content

SDMA gives you three ways to find content. All of them cover every project that you can open, so you do not need to remember where something lives. Choose the one that fits what you know: a name, a property, or a place.

## Go to Anything
<a name="search-go-to-anything"></a>

Use **Go to anything** when you know the name. The box is at the top of most pages. Press Command\+K (macOS) or Ctrl\+K (Windows and Linux) to move to it from anywhere. It matches as you type:
+  **Projects, assets, and files.** Choose one to open it.
+  **Places.** Choose a place to fly to it on the map and see the content there.
+  **Asset templates and connectors.** Choose one to open it in the library settings.

Press Enter to search for the words that you typed on the Search page.

## Search by Property
<a name="search-by-property"></a>

Use the Search page when you know what the content is. It finds assets and files by name, attribute, or metadata. Choose **Search** in the sidebar.

1. Type words to match asset names, file names, and descriptions.

1. To filter by a property, choose one in the search box. The properties are in three groups:
   +  **Assets**: **Asset name**, **Asset size**, **Location**, **Asset status**, **Asset created by**, **Asset creation time**, **Asset updated by**, and **Asset update time**.
   +  **Files**: **File name**, **File type**, **File size**, and **File last modified**.
   +  **Projects**: **Project name**.

1. For a date property, choose a relative range, such as the last day, or a custom range.

1. Add more filters to narrow the results. Between two filters you can choose **and** or **or**. If you also use **Filter by project** or **Filter by template**, filters join with **and**.

1. To search within one project or one asset template, use **Filter by project** or **Filter by template**.

1. Choose a result to open it. In the **Files** table, choose the download icon to download a file from the results.

The attributes that your asset templates define are also properties that you can choose. **Recent searches** are one click away from the history button, so you can run a search again.

## Explore by Map
<a name="search-explore-map"></a>

Use the map when you know where the content is. Choose **Projects** in the sidebar, then **Explore map**. Pan and zoom to any place, or type a place in the box at the top, and the map shows the content located there. Numbers on the map show how many items are in an area.
+  **In this area.** The panel on the right lists the projects, assets, and files in view, with a count for each. Choose an item to open it. Choose **Browse these projects** to see the projects in view as project cards.
+  **Filters.** The panel on the left narrows what the map shows. Choose whether to show projects, assets, or files, then filter by file type, asset template, when it was updated, or who created it. Filters match assets. A project is shown when it holds a matching asset.
