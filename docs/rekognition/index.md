# Amazon Rekognition Documentation

Amazon Rekognition makes it easy to add image and video analysis to your applications. You just provide an image or video to the Amazon Rekognition API, and the service can identify objects, people, text, scenes, and activities. It can detect any inappropriate content as well. Amazon Rekognition also provides highly accurate facial analysis and facial recognition. With Amazon Rekognition Custom Labels, you can create a machine learning model that finds the objects, scenes, and concepts that are specific to your business needs.

## Using Amazon Rekognition

- [Detect face liveness](/rekognition/latest/dg/face-liveness.html): Detect real users and deter bad actors using spoofs in seconds during facial verification.
- [Find labels](/rekognition/latest/dg/labels.html): Detect thousands of objects and scenes, plus activities such as "delivering a package" or "playing soccer" in images, stored videos, and streaming videos.
- [Find custom labels](/rekognition/latest/customlabels-dg/what-is.html): Detect custom objects in images such as brand logos using automated machine learning (AutoML) to train your models with as few as 10 images.
- [Detect and analyze faces](/rekognition/latest/dg/faces.html): Detect faces appearing in images and stored videos and recognize attributes such as open eyes, glasses, and facial hair for each.
- [Compare and search faces](/rekognition/latest/dg/collections.html): Determine the similarity of a face against another picture or from your private image repository.
- [Recognize celebrities](/rekognition/latest/dg/celebrities.html): Identify well-known people to catalog photos and footage for media, marketing, and advertising in images and stored videos.
- [Moderate content](/rekognition/latest/dg/moderation.html): Detect potentially unsafe, inappropriate, or unwanted content across images and stored videos.
- [Detect text](/rekognition/latest/dg/text-detection.html): Extract skewed and distorted text from images and stored videos of street signs, social media posts, and product packaging.
- [Detect video segments](/rekognition/latest/dg/text-detection.html): Detect key segments in stored videos, such as black frames, start or end credits, slates, color bars, and shots.
- [Track people paths](/rekognition/latest/dg/persons.html): Track the path people take in stored videos.
- [Detect personal protective equipment](/rekognition/latest/dg/ppe-detection.html): Improve workplace safety practices by detecing personal protective equipment (PPE) worn by persons in an image.
