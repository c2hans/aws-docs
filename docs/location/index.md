# Amazon Location Service Documentation

Use Amazon Location Service to easily and securely add maps, places, routes, geofences, and trackers, to your applications. With Amazon Location Service, you safeguard customer data, reduce cost, and easily integrate with other AWS services.

## Latest Version

- [Maps](/location/latest/developerguide/maps.html): Amazon Location Maps lets you create beautiful map-enabled visualizations, including embedding interactive dynamic maps, or static map imagery in your apps and even layering your own data on top of the base map.
- [Places](/location/latest/developerguide/places.html): Amazon Location Places enables searching and geocoding of geographic data, including addresses, points of interest, and business info such as categories, food types, brands, and opening hours.
- [Routes](/location/latest/developerguide/routes.html): Amazon Location Routes calculates travel routes, estimate trip time, and optimizes travel itineraries and logistics problems using up-to-date road network data, live traffic, and rich road and vehicle attributes.
- [Geofences](/location/latest/developerguide/geofences.html): Amazon Location Geofences allows setting up and managing virtual boundaries for monitoring when devices enter or leave specific areas, useful for asset tracking and alerts.
- [Trackers](/location/latest/developerguide/trackers.html): Amazon Location Trackers lets you track the location of devices in near real-time, visualize movements on maps, and integrate tracking data with geofences for automated alerts.
- [Authentication](/location/latest/developerguide/access.html): Learn how to authenticate with Amazon Location through API keys, IAM roles, and Amazon Cognito, ensuring secure access to your location-based applications.
