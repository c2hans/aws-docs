# Post an answer to a question in your private re:Post
<a name="post-answer"></a>

You can answer a question that's posted in your private re:Post in AWS re:Post Private.

Follow these steps to post an answer to a question in your private re:Post:

1. On your private re:Post application home page, choose **Questions**.

1. From the list of questions, choose the question that you want to post an answer for.

1. Under **Add your answer**, enter your answer.

1. Choose **Post answer**.

The answer that you added is now displayed under the question.
