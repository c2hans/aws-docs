# Complete your profile
<a name="register-admin-profile"></a>

 When you sign in to your private re:Post for the first time, you must complete your profile.

Follow these steps to complete your profile:

1. For **Screen name**, enter a display name. This name is displayed on all your posts.

1.  For **Email for notifications**, enter your email address.

1.  (Optional) For **First name** and **Last name**, enter your first name and last name.

1.  For **Interface and notifications language**, select a language.

1.  For **Content language**, select additional language options for your content on re:Post Private.

1. Choose **Complete profile**.

After you complete your profile, you receive a verification email. In this email, choose **Verify E-mail**.

In your private re:Post, choose **Continue to service** to start working with your private re:Post.

**Note**
To update your profile, choose your user profile, choose **Settings**, update as needed, and then choose **Update your profile**.
