# Notifications
<a name="notifications"></a>

You can receive a notification in the AWS Supply Chain web application or through email.

To enable notifications, perform the following procedure:

1. In the left navigation pane on the AWS Supply Chain dashboard, choose the **Settings** icon.

   The **Settings** page appears.

1. Choose **Notifications**.

   The **Notification Preferences** page appears.

1. Under **Insights**, slide the **In-app** and **Email** button to receive notifications when a lead time deviation is identified, inventory risks are identified, lead time export fails, or when lead time export succeeds.
**Note**
You can choose to receive an email, in-app notification, or both.

1. Under **Forecast Collaboration**, slide the **In-app** button to receive a notification in AWS Supply Chain when there is an update to the forecast or if the forecast request is decline by the Partner.

   You can also use the **Email** button to receive a summarized email once a day on all the forecast updates.

1. Under **Purchase Orders**, slide the **In-app** button to receive a notification in AWS Supply Chain when there is a purchase order update by the Partner.

   You can also use the **Email** button to receive a summarized email once a day on all the purchase order updates.

1. Under **Disclosure Data Requests**, slide the **In-app** button to receive a notification in AWS Supply Chain when a data request is submitted or declined or to track the status of the data request. For example, in progress, rework requested, canceled, and so on.

1. Choose **Save**.

1. On the AWS Supply Chain dashboard, choose the **Bell** icon on the top-right to view the in-app notifications.
