# Security design principles
<a name="sec-design-principles"></a>

 These design principles help you achieve and maintain security for advertising workloads in the cloud. They extend the [lens-wide design principles](design-principles.md) with guidance specific to protecting advertising data, systems, and assets.
+  **Protect the advertising pipeline:** Control access to and from your demand-side platform (DSP) using identity management and network access control. Protect data in transit using encryption.
+  **Enforce trust boundaries across programmatic partners:** Treat every SSP, DSP, and measurement provider as an independent trust domain. Validate identity, authorize actions, and encrypt data at each boundary crossing rather than relying on network perimeter alone. Per-partner certificates, mutual TLS, and per-partner rate limits create trust boundaries that limit the scope of impact if any single partner relationship allows unintended access.
+  **Build transparency into the supply chain:** Use IAB Tech Lab ads.txt, sellers.json, and SupplyChain as declarations and validation inputs, not as cryptographic authentication. Evaluate that evidence through a decision process with defined cache refreshes, intermediary checks, mismatch handling, and false-positive review. The process can hold or reject transactions, but the standards alone don't prove completeness or guard against fraud.
+  **Design for privacy by default in converged environments:** Use privacy-enhancing technologies (AWS Clean Rooms, confidential computing, differential privacy) to derive advertising value without exposing individual-level data. Under the purpose intersection rule, a converged purpose may proceed only when every contributing source authorizes it. Record what authorizes each source, because consent is one basis among several.
+  **Automate compliance across jurisdictions:** Advertising workloads can fall under data protection laws and regulations such as GDPR, CCPA and CPRA, and COPPA, and can also implement voluntary industry frameworks such as the IAB Europe TCF. Applicability and the resulting obligations depend on territory, data subjects, audience, processing purpose, transfer mechanism, revenue or volume thresholds, and contract. Require privacy or legal owners to assess these obligations before implementation, then encode approved requirements as continuously enforced policy controls. Use IAM permissions boundaries with aws:RequestedRegion conditions, AWS Control Tower controls, and service control policies (SCPs) as authorization guardrails alongside service-specific data-location, inventory, logging, backup, and transfer controls because these guardrails don't prove data residency.
+  **Validate creative integrity before activation:** Malvertising, meaning creatives that run unauthorized code, redirect viewers to unintended destinations, or collect data without authorization, requires automated scanning before any creative enters the ad-serving pipeline. At scale, every programmatic creative must be validated automatically, because manual review can't match the volume and velocity of programmatic advertising. Scan at ingestion time and re-scan active creatives that reference external resources that could change after initial approval.
+  **Protect linear distribution feeds and ad signaling:** When linear broadcast workflows converge with cloud-based ad insertion, SCTE-35 ad break signals move from physically secured broadcast facilities to IP-based delivery over cloud infrastructure. Without integrity controls, unauthorized parties could inject or remove ad markers affecting monetization. Secure the ingest path with encrypted transport (SRT with AES), restrict MediaLive input security groups to known encoder IP ranges, and protect schedule action APIs with IAM policies.
