# Operational excellence design principles
<a name="ops-design-principles"></a>

 These design principles help you achieve and maintain operational excellence for advertising workloads in the cloud. They extend the [lens-wide design principles](design-principles.md) with guidance specific to running, monitoring, and improving advertising operations.
+  **Automate operational processes:** Advertising workloads depend on extensive automation to keep operations consistent, reliable, and scalable. Automating advertising-specific processes reduces the human errors that lead to ad serving failures, data quality problems, or compliance violations. Automation should span adapter onboarding for Prebid Server, SCTE-35 marker validation for SGAI, EPG schedule management for FAST channels, and rendering pipeline orchestration for innovative ad formats.
+  **Optimize for observability:** Advertising workloads generate large volumes of data that must be collected, processed, and analyzed in real time. Observability means deep understanding through metrics, logs, and traces rather than basic monitoring. It helps you identify and resolve issues quickly, improve performance over time, and make data-driven decisions about your advertising business. Observability must cover every layer: monetization, the content delivery pipeline, playout health, and rendering health for innovative ad formats.
+  **Optimize for publisher yield reliability:** Publisher workloads must maintain ongoing monetization across all ad insertion methods. Design operations to detect and remediate yield-affecting issues (fill rate drops, adapter failures, manifest errors) before they affect revenue. Treat revenue continuity as a first-class health indicator alongside infrastructure metrics. Publisher KPIs such as eCPM, fill rate, bid density, identity-signal match rates, and ad break completion rate should be monitored with the same urgency as system uptime.
+  **Automate content-to-monetization workflows:** Publishers operating FAST channels, SGAI pipelines, and innovative ad formats should automate the operational chain from content ingestion through ad insertion to revenue reporting. Automation should span adapter onboarding for Prebid Server, SCTE-35 marker validation for SGAI, EPG schedule management for FAST channels, and rendering pipeline orchestration for innovative ad formats.
+  **Establish performance metrics that connect operations to revenue outcomes:** Define KPIs that correlate technical health to business impact. For publisher workloads this means defining SLOs for yield health (eCPM, fill rate, bids per auction) and delivery quality (ad break completion, channel uptime, engagement rates) with error budgets that trigger change freezes when exhausted. SLOs that remain green while monetization degrades are insufficient, because operational health must be measured in revenue terms.
+  **Foster a publisher operations culture that prioritizes yield reliability:** Publisher organizations should build a culture where yield degradation is treated with the same urgency as system availability. Shared dashboards visible to both engineering and ad operations, defined severity classifications for revenue-affecting anomalies, and weekly yield review meetings make monetization health a shared organizational priority rather than a business-only concern.
+  **Continuously evolve through feedback loops and experimentation:** Establish systematic feedback loops that inform your optimization efforts through yield data. Conduct regular game days to identify operational gaps before production incidents. Use A/B testing frameworks to safely experiment with new ad formats and auction configurations. Conduct blameless post-incident reviews for all revenue-affecting events with quantified revenue impact and tracked improvement actions.
