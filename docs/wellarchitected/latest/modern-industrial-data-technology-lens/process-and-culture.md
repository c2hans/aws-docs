# Process and culture
<a name="process-and-culture"></a>

| MIDASUS06: How do you integrate sustainability into manufacturing processes and culture? |
| --- |
|   |

 Manufacturing organizations can promote sustainability through effective processes and cultural change by establishing clear sustainability goals and implementing continuous improvement processes. Building a sustainability-oriented culture requires not just technological solutions, but also organizational alignment, education, and metrics that make environmental impact visible and actionable across the enterprise.
