# Responsibilities
<a name="support-responsibilities"></a>

The following are the Amazon DocumentDB responsibilities and your responsibilities with Amazon DocumentDB Extended Support.

**Amazon DocumentDB responsibilities**

After the Amazon DocumentDB version 3.6 end of standard support date, Amazon DocumentDB will apply patches, bug fixes, and upgrades for version 3.6 clusters enrolled in Extended Support.

**Your responsibilities**

You are responsible for upgrading your engine to a newer engine version before Amazon DocumentDB version 3.6's end of Extended Support date. If you do not upgrade your cluster, after the Amazon DocumentDB version 3.6 end of Extended Support date, Amazon DocumentDB will attempt to upgrade your cluster to the latest engine version that's supported under Amazon DocumentDB standard support.
