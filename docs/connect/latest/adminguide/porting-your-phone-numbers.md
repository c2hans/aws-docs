# Porting your phone numbers from your carrier to Connect Customer
<a name="porting-your-phone-numbers"></a>

Porting phone numbers from your existing carrier to Connect Customer is a multi-step process. It's important to get started several months in advance of your scheduled go-live date, and have all of your documentation in order.
