# Rules are applied to new contacts when conversational analytics analyzes conversations
<a name="rules-applied-to-new-contacts"></a>

After you add rules, they are applied to new contacts that occur after the rule was added. Rules are applied when Connect Customer conversational analytics analyzes conversations.

You cannot apply rules to past, stored conversations.
