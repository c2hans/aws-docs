# Configure third-party speech providers in Connect Customer
<a name="configure-third-party-speech-providers"></a>

You can configure third-party speech-to-text (STT) and text-to-speech (TTS) providers in Connect Customer to expand language coverage, improve recognition accuracy, and deliver more expressive synthesized speech. This section describes how to configure third-party STT providers for bots and third-party TTS providers for use in contact flows.
