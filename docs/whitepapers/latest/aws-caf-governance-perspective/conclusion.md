# Conclusion
<a name="conclusion"></a>

 Governance is a critical ingredient of successful cloud transformation efforts. Effective governance will help you accelerate and orchestrate your cloud initiatives while maximizing organizational benefits and minimizing transformation-related risks. Implementing the seven capabilities described in this paper will help you identify and remove blockers, reach alignment on goals, progress, and achievements, and ultimately accelerate organizational change.
