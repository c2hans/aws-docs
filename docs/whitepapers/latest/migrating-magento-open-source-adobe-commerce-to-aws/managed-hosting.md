# Managed hosting
<a name="managed-hosting"></a>

 For customers who are not comfortable or do not have the resources to manage their own deployment of Magento Open Source or Adobe Commerce on Cloud Infrastructure Self-Service on AWS, there are several companies that specialize in providing managed hosting deployments of Magento Open Source or Adobe Commerce on Cloud Infrastructure Self-Service on AWS. These companies, such as [CloudHesive](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/find/partnerdetails/?n=CloudHesive&id=001E000000qK5f6IAC), take care of the aspects of deploying, securing, patching, and maintaining Magento. Some also provide design services and custom development for Magento storefronts. You can use [AWS Partner Finder](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/find/) to find and compare providers that specialize Magento Open Source or Adobe Commerce on Cloud Infrastructure Self-Service hosting.
