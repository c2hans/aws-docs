# Moving from the legacy experience to the simplified experience

Your IAM Identity Center instance uses the legacy experience until you turn on the simplified experience. Turning on the simplified experience doesn't disrupt your existing applications, grants, service native access controls, or sign-in flows; they continue to work. For the behavior changes, see [How the simplified experience differs from the legacy experience](simplified-tip-overview.md#simplified-tip-how-it-differs).

To move to the simplified experience:

1. Review [Prerequisites and considerations for the simplified experience](simplified-tip-prerequisites.md).

1. [Enable the simplified experience](simplified-tip-enabling.md).
