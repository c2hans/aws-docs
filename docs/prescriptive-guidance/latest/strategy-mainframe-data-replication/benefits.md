# Business benefits of replicating mainframe data to AWS
<a name="benefits"></a>

Strategic motivations drive the decision to replicate mainframe data to the AWS Cloud. These motivations aim to enhance business agility, innovation, and resilience. They include the following:
+ **Agility and innovation** – In fast-paced business environments, agility and innovation help you stay ahead of the competition. By replicating data to the AWS Cloud, enterprises can unlock new opportunities for innovation, accelerate time-to-market for new products and services, and respond swiftly to changing customer needs and market dynamics.
+ **Scalability and flexibility** – Traditional mainframe systems often face limitations in scalability and flexibility. This hinders their ability to adapt to evolving business requirements. Data replication to the AWS Cloud offers unparalleled scalability and flexibility. Enterprises can scale resources up or down as needed, accommodate fluctuating workloads, and experiment with new business models and technologies.
+ **Cost optimization** – Cost optimization is a key priority for enterprises that seek to maximize the value of their IT investments and minimize operational expenses. Data replication to the AWS Cloud allows organizations to use pay-as-you-go pricing models. These models reduce upfront capital expenditures and optimize resource utilization, leading to significant cost savings over time.
+ **Resilience and business continuity** –Resilience and business continuity in the face of disruptions, whether caused by natural disasters, cyberattacks, or system failures, is essential for safeguarding business operations and maintaining customer trust. Data replication to the AWS Cloud provides built-in redundancy, disaster recovery capabilities, and geographic diversification. Data replication can provide continuous availability of critical data and applications.

Data replication to the AWS Cloud offers benefits that align with these strategic motivations, including the following:
+ **Enhanced business agility** – Enables rapid deployment of new capabilities, seamless integration with emerging technologies, and faster response to market opportunities.
+ **Improved scalability and flexibility** – Provides on-demand access to scalable resources, enabling dynamic resource allocation and supporting business growth.
+ **Cost savings** – Optimizes infrastructure costs, reduces capital expenditures, and eliminates the need for costly hardware refresh cycles.
+ **Enhanced resilience** – Bolsters resilience and business continuity through redundant data storage, automated failover, and disaster recovery capabilities.
+ **Empowered decision-making** – Facilitates real-time access to accurate and actionable insights, empowering informed decision-making and driving business innovation.
