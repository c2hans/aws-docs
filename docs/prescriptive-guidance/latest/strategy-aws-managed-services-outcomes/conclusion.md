# Next steps and resources
<a name="conclusion"></a>

Use AWS Managed Services (AMS) to perform undifferentiated, yet important cloud infrastructure and security operational activities. By using AMS, your cloud operations teams can focus on learning and using in-demand technology skills, such as CI/CD and artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML), instead of spending their time on mundane operational tasks. Providing cloud operations teams the opportunity to learn and use in-demand technology skills through AMS can also improve staff retention.

IT leaders can use AMS to achieve operational excellence in the cloud, from day one. At the same time, you can reduce the total cost of ownership (TCO) for cloud operations, improve application security and reliability, and achieve faster times to market. AMS can help organizations focus on their core business and competition, respond quickly to changing business needs, and achieve their desired business goals and objectives in time. AMS fits well into the operating models defined in the AWS Well-Architected Framework. It helps you transition rapidly from traditional, [fully separated](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/fully-separated-operating-model.html) operating models to a strategic one with [centralized governance and a service provider](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/operational-excellence-pillar/separated-aeo-and-ieo-with-centralized-governance-and-a-service-provider.html).

To get started with AMS, contact your AWS representative or reach out to [AMS](https://pages.awscloud.com/AWS-Managed-Services.html)[ sales](https://pages.awscloud.com/AWS-Managed-Services.html) directly. In addition, we recommend that you review the following resources, which provide additional information about AMS.
