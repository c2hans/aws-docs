# Objectives
+ Enhanced Citizen Engagement - Implement secure, compliant messaging channels to communicate critical information to citizens through trusted, verified channels. Short codes and 10DLC provide recognizable, consistent sender identities that build trust and improve engagement rates.
+ Improved Service Delivery - Deploy omnichannel communication strategies that reach citizens through their preferred channels. Combine [Amazon Connect](https://aws.amazon.com/products/connect/?trk=25b81a8c-c9b1-4866-9ba5-f9cb1c5e792a&sc_channel=ps) and [AWS End User Messaging SMS](https://aws.amazon.com/end-user-messaging/) to create seamless experiences across voice and messaging touchpoints.
+ Regulatory Compliance - Meet federal requirements for citizen communications with properly registered, approved messaging channels that comply with telecommunications regulations, including TCPA, CTIA guidelines, and carrier policies.
+ Cost Optimization - Replace expensive, fragmented communication systems with scalable AWS solutions that provide predictable pricing and eliminate hardware maintenance costs while improving deliverability.
+ Operational Efficiency - Automate routine communications and notifications to reduce manual processes, allowing staff to focus on higher-value activities while ensuring consistent citizen experiences.
+ Crisis Communication Readiness - Establish pre-approved, high-throughput communication channels for emergency notifications and critical updates, ensuring timely information delivery during urgent situations.
