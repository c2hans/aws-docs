# Best practices for migrations
<a name="best-practices"></a>

Migrating to Amazon Connect Customer is likely to change your contact center's technical architecture and your staff's daily processes. To minimize disruptions, follow the best practices in this section when you design and build your new contact center.
+ [Technical considerations](tech-considerations.md)
+ [Operational considerations](ops-considerations.md)
