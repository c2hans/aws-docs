# Phase 1: Prioritize - Define the path
<a name="phase-1"></a>

This phase involves rapid discovery for organizational transformation readiness, interactive sessions to align leaders and business priorities, and design sessions to develop the transformation strategy, value at stake, and high-level roadmap. It is illustrated in the following diagram.

![Focus areas and outcomes of the Prioritize phase of enterprise transformations.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/strategy-enterprise-transformation/images/guide-img/2d221fda-ae0d-490b-b51b-592f55e8b0d3/images/8efc2676-069f-4bb7-9849-5ca073cd0302.png)

By the end of this phase, organizations establish a solid foundation for their transformation journey and document their roadmap. Business priorities are set; transformation readiness discovery is completed, inclusive of business value, financial operations, cloud operating model, culture, and people; and the high-level transformation strategy and roadmap are built.

Key activities:
+ Set the foundation and determine unified business objectives with KPIs.
+ Use the AWS Transformation Readiness Diagnostic, which is a tool that benchmarks companies against enterprise transformation best practices and organizational capabilities, and helps uncover opportunities for driving value from the cloud. (For more information about this tool, contact the [AWS Professional Services team](https://pages.awscloud.com/AWS-Professional-Services.html).)
+ Confirm business and financial strategies.
+ Define a path to a strategic target state by aligning leaders, cloud operating model, and financial management capabilities to business priorities.
+ Define the transformation plan.
