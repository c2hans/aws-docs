# Costs and licenses
<a name="costs-licenses"></a>

Every organization has its own unique data analytics requirements. Teradata VantageCloud Enterprise offers a choice of [cloud pricing models](https://www.teradata.com/Getting-Started/Pricing) so that you can find the right fit for your workloads, budget, and goals.

For more information about licensing, contact your [Teradata account team](https://www.teradata.com/About-Us/Contact).
