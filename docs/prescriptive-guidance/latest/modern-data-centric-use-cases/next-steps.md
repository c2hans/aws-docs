# Next steps
<a name="next-steps"></a>

We recommend that you take the following next steps:

1. Assess your current data engineering process and contact data experts for further guidance and assistance.

1. Adjust your data engineering process to ensure that all the stages of the data lifecycle are optimized for efficiency.

1. Use the data engineering principles and best practices in this guide to design a data-centric architecture and choose the AWS services that are most appropriate for your use case.
