# Benefits of Matter certification for smart-home consumers

Matter provides a common language that helps smart-home devices work together across major smart-home brands and services. When devices are certified with Matter, consumers can set up and manage their smart homes more simply, and they have greater flexibility and choice in how they control their devices.

## Simplified setup and unified management
<a name="setup-management"></a>

A common frustration for consumers is the complex setup and onboarding process required to operate different smart-home devices and get them to work together. Each device might require its own proprietary app and separate account. To address this issue, Matter enables plug-and-play functionality for certified devices. To onboard a Matter-certified device, you connect the device to the local home network, and then use a Matter admin application, such as the Amazon Alexa app, to scan the QR code on the device. Matter version 1.4.1 introduced Enhanced Setup Flow, which supports multi-device QR codes and NFC onboarding to further streamline the process.

This unified setup experience through a single app means consumers no longer need to use multiple, separate apps to manage different brands of devices. They can view and control their Matter-certified lights, locks, sensors, thermostats, cameras, appliances, energy management devices, and more from a single interface. Users of Apple HomeKit, Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, and Samsung SmartThings can discover and control Matter devices without downloading separate manufacturer apps. Managing smart-home devices through a unified system reduces complexity for consumers and simplifies building and expanding their setup.

## Improved choice and flexibility in voice control
<a name="choice-voice-control"></a>

Voice control has become a popular way for consumers to interact with their smart-home devices. However, today the choice of voice assistant often dictates what brands of devices you can control with your voice. Matter changes this by enabling voice control across device brands and voice assistants.

Consumers gain the flexibility to choose the voice assistant that suits their needs, with fewer device compatibility concerns. A user comfortable with Google Assistant can control their Matter-certified devices with their voice, even if the devices were originally manufactured for Amazon Alexa or Apple HomeKit.

This cross-compatibility of voice control gives users greater choice. Users can pick devices based on features and pricing instead of compatibility with a single voice assistant. If a user wants to change voice assistants in the future, their existing smart-home setup can move with them because all devices use the common Matter standard.

Matter version 1.4 introduced the Enhanced Multi-Admin feature, which lets multiple voice assistants control a single device simultaneously. Family members can use their preferred voice assistant (Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant, or Apple Siri) to control the same devices with minimal additional setup.
