# Next steps
<a name="next-steps"></a>

After you finish reviewing this guide, we recommend that you take the following next steps to implement MACO:

1. **Contact a MACO expert.** A MACO expert can help answer your questions and address your concerns. If you're already working with an AWS account team, contact the team and request help from a MACO expert. If you don't have an account team, contact optimize-microsoft@amazon.com.

1. **Apply the recommendations.** Use the best practices and strategies that you learned in this guide and from speaking with a MACO expert.

1. **Track cost changes. **Tag your workloads and use services such as AWS Cost Explorer and AWS Budgets for detailed cost tracking, monitoring, and control.
