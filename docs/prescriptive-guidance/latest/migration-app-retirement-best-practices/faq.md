# FAQ
<a name="faq"></a>

## Why should I have a methodical process for decommissioning assets?
<a name="methodical-process"></a>

First, a methodical process helps you take the necessary steps to properly retire the asset. This includes backing up data, removing the asset from any networks or systems it's connected to, and properly disposing of the asset. Second, a methodical process can help to prevent mistakes or oversights that could lead to data breaches or other security issues. Finally, having a clear process in place can help to make the decommissioning process more efficient and cost-effective.

## What happens if I switch off an application without notifying anyone?
<a name="switch-off-app"></a>

If you switch off an application without notifying anyone, it could cause a number of problems. First, if you shut down a mission-critical application without warning, you can cause major disruptions to your organization. This could result in lost productivity, missed opportunities, or other negative consequences. Second, if the application is connected to other systems or networks, shutting it down without warning could cause disruptions or even damage to those systems. Finally, if the application contains sensitive or confidential data, shutting it down without warning could put that data at risk of being accessed by unauthorized users. For these reasons, it's important to have a methodical process in place for decommissioning applications, and to notify the appropriate stakeholders before shutting them down.

## How can an effective decommissioning process help me realize the business value of a cloud migration?
<a name="business-value"></a>

An effective decommissioning process can help you realize the business value of a cloud migration in several ways. First, it can help you retire on-premises assets in an orderly and efficient manner, which can help minimize downtime and avoid disruptions to the business during the migration process. Second, an effective decommissioning process can help to ensure that all necessary data is migrated to the cloud and that any remaining data is properly backed up or disposed of. That means you can take full advantage of the benefits of the cloud, such as increased scalability and accessibility. Finally, an effective decommissioning process can help to reduce the overall costs of the migration by ensuring that you're only retiring and replacing assets that are necessary for the move to the cloud.
