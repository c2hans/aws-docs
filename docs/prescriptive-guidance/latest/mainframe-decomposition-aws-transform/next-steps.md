# Next steps and resources
<a name="next-steps"></a>

After you've become familiar with the process described in this guide, we recommend that you contact your AWS account team and schedule a consultation with mainframe modernization experts.

To plan your first project:

1. Choose a candidate application.

1. Gather your source code.

1. Define clear success criteria.

To get started with AWS Transform:

1. [Set up ](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/transform/latest/userguide/transform-setup.html)AWS Transform.

1. Try out AWS Transform by following the steps in the [quick start](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/transform/latest/userguide/transform-setup.html#transform-app-admin-starting-standalone).

1. Begin your first decomposition analysis.
