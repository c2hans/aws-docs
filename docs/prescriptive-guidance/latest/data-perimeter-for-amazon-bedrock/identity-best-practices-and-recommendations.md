# Best practices and recommendations
<a name="identity-best-practices-and-recommendations"></a>

1. **Principle of least privilege** – Grant only the minimum permissions required for each use case.

1. **Regular access reviews** – Conduct quarterly reviews of cross-account access and model permissions.

1. **Automated policy testing** – Implement automated tests to verify policy effectiveness.

1. **Documentation** – Maintain clear documentation of all identity perimeter exceptions and their business justifications.

1. **Gradual rollout** – Implement identity controls incrementally, starting with the most sensitive workloads.
