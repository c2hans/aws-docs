# Overcoming common cloud transformation challenges
<a name="introduction"></a>

*Ashish Sharma and Ravindra Vaidya, Amazon Web Services*

"Cloud is the future." Businesses have been hearing this phrase for more than 20 years. For most organizations, cloud adoption is essential to remain relevant. Cloud services have reached a high degree of maturity and are integral to operating a successful business. Many businesses view a move to the cloud as a simple *rehost *migration, also known as a *lift and shift *migration, where you move an application to the cloud without making any changes to take advantage of cloud capabilities. However, cloud adoption can provide a deeper transformation for the organization because cloud-native features can improve agility, performance, and scalability. Without such a transformation, cloud workloads can become just another variant of the existing, on-premises infrastructure.

This guide focuses on the key challenges that enterprises face during their cloud adoption journey and includes recommendations to address those challenges. It also highlights potential solutions through the lens of the [AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF)](https://aws.amazon.com/cloud-adoption-framework/). AWS CAF identifies specific organizational capabilities that support successful cloud transformations. The capabilities are grouped into six areas of focus that are called *perspectives*. The AWS CAF perspectives are: business, people, governance, platform, security, and operations. Each perspective covers distinct responsibilities owned or managed by functionally related stakeholders. In general, the business, people, and governance perspectives focus on business capabilities; whereas the platform, security, and operations perspectives focus on technical capabilities. In this guide, the perspectives are used to provide a structured approach for discussing cloud adoption challenges and solutions.
