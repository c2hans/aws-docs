# Grassroots chaos engineering initiatives
<a name="grassroots-chaos-engineering"></a>

The chaos engineering journey often starts at the grassroots level, where engineering teams identify needs and begin experimenting with chaos engineering independently.

In this grassroots approach, teams experiment, learn, and refine their chaos engineering practices. The value of chaos engineering can be demonstrated through the following tangible results:
+ Reduced incidents
+ Better observability
+ Faster recovery times
+ Improved and continual system resilience

Grassroots chaos engineering initiatives typically emerge under specific organizational conditions. They require an environment with a high degree of engineering autonomy, where teams have the freedom to experiment and innovate without excessive bureaucratic barriers. Local expertise in resilience engineering or distributed systems is crucial, because it provides the technical foundation for understanding and implementing chaos experiments. Most importantly, these initiatives often rely on *chaos champions*—passionate individuals who understand the value of chaos engineering. Chaos champions are willing to advocate for chaos engineering adoption, educate their peers, and drive initial experiments. Without organizational freedom, technical expertise, and motivated champions, grassroots chaos engineering efforts rarely take root, regardless of their potential benefits.
