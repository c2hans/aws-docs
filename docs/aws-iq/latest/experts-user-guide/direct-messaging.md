# Direct messaging
<a name="direct-messaging"></a>

You can receive direct messages from customers through your individual or company profile. If you have provided your individual and company details in the **Account Settings**, you can respond using your individual name or the name of your company.

You can view customer messages in the **Conversations** tab. Select the message to respond.
