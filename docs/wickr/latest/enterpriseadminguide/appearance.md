# Appearance
<a name="appearance"></a>

Super administrators can manage and customize the appearance of Wickr for their network.

**To customize the appearance of Wickr**

1. In the navigation pane of the Wickr Super Administrator Console, choose **Appearance**.

1. On the **Appearance** page, choose **Edit** in the top right corner of the **Customize login screen** section.

1. Under **Add a custom logo**, choose **Upload Image** to upload a logo image.

1. In the **Add a custom text** dialog box, enter a description for your admin console login screen.

1. Choose **Save**.
