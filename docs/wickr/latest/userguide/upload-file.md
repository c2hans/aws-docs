# Upload a file in the Wickr client
<a name="upload-file"></a>

Users in self-moderated group conversations and moderators of room conversations can upload a file to a room in the Wickr client.

To upload a file to a room, complete the following steps.

1. Sign in to the Wickr client. For more information, see [Sign in to the Wickr client](getting-started.md#sign-in-step2).

1. In the navigation pane, choose the room or group where you want to upload a file.

1. Choose the **Files** tab at the top of the room in Wickr client.

1. Under the **Files** tab, to upload a file into a folder, select the folder name where you want to upload your file.

1. On desktop, choose **Upload** to upload your file.

   On mobile, choose the **\+** icon at the bottom of the screen, and then choose **Upload File** to upload your file.
**Note**
Users can only view folders and files under the **Files** tab.
