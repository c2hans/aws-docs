# Creating pricing plans
<a name="create-pricingplan"></a>

Use the following steps to create a pricing plan (customer managed).

**To create a pricing plan**

1. Open AWS Billing Conductor at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/billingconductor/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/billingconductor/).

1. In the navigation pane, choose **Pricing configuration**.

1. From the **Pricing plan** tab, choose **Create pricing plan**.

1. For **Pricing plan details**, enter the name of the pricing plan. For naming restrictions, see [Quotas and restrictions](limits.md).

1. (Optional) For **Description**, enter a description for the pricing plan.

1. In the **Pricing rules table**, choose the pricing rules that you want to be associated with the pricing plan. You can filter the pricing rules by pricing rule name, scope, details, type, or rate.

1. Choose **Create pricing plan**.
