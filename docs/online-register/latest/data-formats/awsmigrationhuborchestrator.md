| <a name="migrationhub-orchestrator-GetWorkflowStepGroup"></a>[https://docs.aws.amazon.com/migrationhub-orchestrator/latest/APIReference/API_GetWorkflowStepGroup.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/migrationhub-orchestrator/latest/APIReference/API_GetWorkflowStepGroup.html) | Get details of a step group associated with a workflow | Read |
