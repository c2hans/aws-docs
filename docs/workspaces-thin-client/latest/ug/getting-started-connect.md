# Connect your WorkSpaces Thin Client device
<a name="getting-started-connect"></a>

To use your WorkSpaces Thin Client device, you must have a keyboard, mouse, and monitor.

![WorkSpaces Thin Client device with numbered USB ports and power connection.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/hub-req-connect.jpg)

![Device connection panel with HDMI, IR extender, power, USB, and network ports.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/thin-client-back-connect.jpg)

| Number | Device | Instruction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ![Orange computer mouse icon with a white outline and scroll wheel.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/mouse-symbol.jpg)  | Connect your mouse to a USB-A port on your hub. |
| 2 | ![Orange keyboard icon with white grid pattern representing keys.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/keyboard-symbol.jpg)  | Connect your keyboard to a USB-A port on your hub. |
| 3 | ![Orange electrical plug icon representing a power connection or energy source.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/plug-symbol.jpg)  | Connect your hub power adapter to the power port on your hub. |
| 4 |  ![Orange outline of a computer monitor icon representing a display or screen.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/monitor-symbol.jpg)  | Connect the HDMI port on your monitor to the HDMI-Out port on your WorkSpaces Thin Client with an HDMI cable (not included). |
| 5 | ![Orange USB icon with the letter B, representing a bootable USB drive.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/usb-symbol.jpg)  | Connect your hub’s USB-A plug to the USB port on your WorkSpaces Thin Client. |
| 6 | ![](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/plug-symbol.jpg)  | Connect your WorkSpaces Thin Client power adapter to the power port on your WorkSpaces Thin Client. |
| 7 |  ![Orange Ethernet cable with connectors at both ends, coiled in a circular shape.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/workspaces-thin-client/latest/ug/images/ethernet-cable-org.jpg)  | (Optional) Connect an Ethernet cable. A network connection is required. If you prefer a wired Ethernet connection instead of Wi-Fi, connect the Ethernet cable (not included) to the network port.  |
