# Setting up your Amazon WorkSpaces Thin Client service
<a name="setting-up"></a>

Your WorkSpaces Thin Client device is ready. You can now turn it on for the first time.

Because this is the first time you're turning on your WorkSpaces Thin Client device, you can follow a basic process to connect your new device to the service.

Your WorkSpaces Thin Client device guides you through this process.
