# Using a Plugable USB docking station
<a name="using-docking-station"></a>

Your WorkSpaces Thin Client device is compatible with a Plugable USB docking station.

For specifications on the docking station, see [UD-3900Z USB Docking Station](usb-hub-specifications.md#ud-usb-hub).

For instructions on connecting peripherals to your WorkSpaces Thin Client device, see [Connect optional WorkSpaces Thin Client devices](getting-started-optional.md)

**Setting up the USB docking station**

1. Connect the one of the HDMI ports from the hub to the second monitor.
**Note**
Only 1 HDMI port can be used with WorkSpaces Thin Client at a time.

1. Connect any required peripherals to the USB ports on the back or front of the docking station.

1. Connect the WorkSpaces Thin Client device to the docking station.

1. Connect the power cable to the docking station.

1. Turn on the power on to the docking station.
