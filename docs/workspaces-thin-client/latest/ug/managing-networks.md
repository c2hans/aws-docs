# Managing networks
<a name="managing-networks"></a>

Your WorkSpaces Thin Client device needs to be connected to a Wi-Fi network in order to access your virtual desktop. You can manage your Wi-Fi networks from your WorkSpaces Thin Client device. Once you connect to a network, your device will automatically sign in to that network when you turn it on.

Notifications about your network are available should you want them. For more information on network notifications, see [Enabling network alerts](alerts-switch.md#network-alerts).
