# Amazon Chime SDK Voice Connector supported use cases
<a name="voice-connector-use-cases"></a>

Amazon Chime SDK Voice Connector is a SIP trunking service for connecting **existing enterprise telephony infrastructure** to AWS.

Enterprise PBX SIP trunking
Connecting your existing on-premises PBX or SBC to the PSTN through AWS for inbound and outbound employee calling

Multisite office connectivity
Routing calls between multiple office locations through SIP

Conference room and meeting room audio
Providing PSTN connectivity for physical meeting spaces

Meeting dial-in and dial-out
Adding phone number participation to Amazon Chime SDK meeting sessions

**Note**
**Key characteristic:** Amazon Chime SDK Voice Connector is intended for **direct use by the organization that owns the AWS account**, providing PSTN connectivity for your own business operations. Amazon Chime SDK Voice Connector does not support PSTN resale, multi-level ownership of phone numbers, or providing telephony services to third parties on behalf of others.
