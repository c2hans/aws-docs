# At a glance
<a name="at-a-glance"></a>

| Category | Amazon Chime SDK Voice Connector | Amazon Connect Customer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | Session Initiation Protocol (SIP) trunking service that connects your existing phone systems to the public switched telephone network (PSTN) | Agentic AI solution that helps enterprises deliver exceptional customer experiences at every touchpoint |
| Designed for | Enterprise telephony infrastructure (Private Branch Exchange (PBX) connectivity, SIP routing) | Customer interactions (support, sales, agentic self-service, AI agents) |
| You provide | Your own PBX, Session Border Controller (SBC), and call handling logic | Configuration only. The platform is fully managed for you |
| Pricing | Per-minute SIP trunking rates | Per-minute or per-message, no per-seat licensing |
