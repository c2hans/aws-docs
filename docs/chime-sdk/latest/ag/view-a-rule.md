# Viewing a SIP rule
<a name="view-a-rule"></a>

Other administrators can view your SIP rules, including their details, and you can do the same with their rules.

**To view a SIP rule**

1. Open the Amazon Chime SDK console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/chime-sdk/home](https://console.aws.amazon.com/chime-sdk/home).

1. In the navigation pane, under **PSTN Audio**, choose **SIP rules**.

   The **SIP rules** page appears and displays all the rules in your organization.

1. To view a rule's details, choose the rule's name.
