# Decision guide
<a name="decision-guide"></a>

To find the right service for your use case, answer the following questions:

1. Will a customer or consumer call your business and interact with an automated system or agent?

   **Yes: Amazon Connect Customer**

1. Does your solution require routing calls to agents, queues, or departments?

   **Yes: Amazon Connect Customer**

1. Does your solution require agentic self-service, voice prompts, or automated call handling?

   **Yes: Amazon Connect Customer**

1. Does your solution require conversational analytics, transcription, or quality monitoring?

   **Yes: Amazon Connect Customer**

1. Does your solution require AI agents, virtual receptionists, or agentic voice for customers?

   **Yes: Amazon Connect Customer**

1. Are you connecting an existing enterprise PBX to the PSTN, with no customer-facing automated handling?

   **Yes: Amazon Chime SDK Voice Connector**

1. Are you providing PSTN connectivity for meeting rooms or internal office telephony only?

   **Yes: Amazon Chime SDK Voice Connector**
