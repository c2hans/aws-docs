# Finding a marketing agency with Agency Connect

Agency Connect lists marketing agencies that AWS approved to run MDF-funded campaigns.

When you don't have the marketing staff or the channel expertise, an agency can plan, build, and run a campaign for you. You can contact an agency before your business plan is approved, and MDF can co-fund approved engagements.
