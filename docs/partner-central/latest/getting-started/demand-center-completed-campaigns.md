# Understanding completed campaigns

A campaign is **Draft** while no asset is live, **Live** once at least one asset is live, and **Completed** after its end date passes in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). A completed campaign is fixed, so its draft assets can't be published. Completion doesn't stop anything that's already running: live assets stay live, and scheduled email messages and posts still send.
