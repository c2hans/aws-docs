# Cloning opportunities
<a name="cloning-opportunities"></a>

You can start creating a new opportunity by cloning any existing opportunity that is not in draft status.

1. On the **Opportunities** page, choose the **Opportunity ID** of the opportunity you want to clone.

1. On the opportunity detail page, choose **Clone**.

1. Edit the information as necessary to create the new opportunity.

1. Review the opportunity details and choose **Save & and Submit**.

**Note**
If you receive a data-entry error message, you can edit and resubmit the opportunity.
