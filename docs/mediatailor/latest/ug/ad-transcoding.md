# Ad transcoding in AWS Elemental MediaTailor
<a name="ad-transcoding"></a>

AWS Elemental MediaTailor transcodes ads, bumpers, and slate to match the format and specifications of your origin video streams, ensuring seamless playback during ad breaks. MediaTailor supports two transcoding approaches: dynamic transcoding (the default) and a custom transcode profile. Understanding the differences between these approaches will help you determine the right configuration for your workflows.

**Topics**
+ [Dynamic transcoding](dynamic-transcoding.md)
+ [Custom transcode profiles](custom-transcode-profiles.md)
