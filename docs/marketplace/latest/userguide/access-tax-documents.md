# Access tax documents

You can access your tax documents, such as 1099 forms, from the **Tax and invoices** page in AWS Partner Central.

**To access your tax documents**

1. Sign in to AWS Partner Central at [https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home).

1. In the left navigation pane, under **Settings**, choose **Tax and invoices**.

1. On the **Primary tax details** tab, find the form that you need:
   + **1099-K** – In the **Tax interview** section, under **Document**, choose the document (for example, 1099K) to download it.
   + **DAC7** – In the **DAC7 questionnaire** section, under **Document**, choose the document (DAC7 questionnaire) to download it.

**Note**
Tax documents are only available after you have completed sales transactions and the relevant tax reporting periods have ended. Forms are typically available for download during tax season.

**Sellers in India**
AWS Marketplace Operations emails GST tax invoices and Seller Listing Fee tax invoices to sellers in India, usually within 2 weeks of the transaction. For questions, contact the AWS Marketplace support team on the [AWS Partner Central and Marketplace Support page](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/support?region=us-east-1) in the AWS Partner Central console.
