# Preparing your multi-product solution
<a name="multi-product-solutions"></a>

## What are multi-product solutions?
<a name="what-are-multi-product-solutions"></a>

 Multi-product solutions let you bundle AWS Marketplace products into a single offering. This makes it easier for customers to find and buy related products together.

 AWS Marketplace multi-product solutions let you combine AWS Marketplace products and services into one solution. This helps customers discover your products and makes transactions simpler. You can showcase complete offerings that solve specific customer problems.

 Multi-product solutions provide two key capabilities:
+ **Solution listings**: Create dedicated solution pages that showcase how multiple AWS Marketplace products work together to solve customer problems.
+ **Offer sets**: Process groups of private offers as a single transaction. This makes it easier for customers to buy multiple products from your solution.

 You can use one or both capabilities depending on your business needs. Solution listings help customers find your integrated offerings. Offer sets make buying easier when selling to specific customers.
