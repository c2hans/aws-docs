# Administering Amazon Quick
<a name="administering-quick"></a>

The administration experience for Amazon Quick depends on how your account was created. Choose the guide that matches your account type:
+ **Free, Plus, and Max accounts** – For accounts created with email or social on [aws.com/quick](https://aws.com/quick). See [Administering Amazon Quick (Free/Plus/Max)](standalone-admin-guide.md).
+ **Enterprise accounts** – For accounts provisioned through the AWS Management Console or AWS CLI. These accounts provide governance controls and the full range of enterprise capabilities, including connections to additional enterprise applications, data sources, and knowledge bases. Access is managed through IAM permissions, and customers can connect to Amazon Quick using their enterprise SSO and identity provider. See [Administration for Amazon Quick](qsysadmin.md).
